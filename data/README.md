# Input data

This archive does not redistribute third-party data. The files below are
fetched from their original providers by `scripts/download_data.sh`, which
verifies every file against `data/CHECKSUMS.sha256`. Each dataset remains
under its provider's terms, which are summarized here and are separate from
this repository's code license.

What *is* tracked under `data/`:

| Path | Origin |
|---|---|
| `*/DESCRIPTION.md`, `yeast-byxrm/MANIFEST.tsv` | Written for this project: dataset notes, retrieval records, checksums |
| `arapheno/metadata/accession_harmonization_manual.csv` | Written for this project: three manual accession-name fixes |
| `processed/arapheno_study27/feature_metadata.tsv`, `study27_mean_trl_top_hits.tsv` | Derived for this project: the feature table of the frozen Study 27 panel and the top-hit list used to select its 200 SNPs |

## Derived Study 27 panel (rebuilt, not tracked)

`processed/arapheno_study27/fixed_panel.npz`, the frozen 81-accession by 203-feature matrix
(200 SNPs plus three kinship PCs, with the Study 27 "Mean(TRL) C" phenotype) used by
`scripts/run_study27_fixed_pc_baseline.py` and `scripts/run_study27_training_groupshap.py`,
is a derived subset of 1001 Genomes genotypes and is therefore not redistributed either.
Rebuild it after `scripts/download_data.sh` (which must extract the genotype matrix):

```bash
python scripts/build_study27_fixed_panel.py --expect-digest 6c60311dd0a02463ed71c691a6a90b02346fd4d55ec06d90260701d951522f62
```

The digest covers the float32 panel, phenotypes, accessions, SNP indices and feature names,
which rebuild bit-for-bit. The float64 PC columns can differ at the 1e-15 level between
LAPACK builds, so they are checked by tolerance rather than digest.

## Derived Arabidopsis fold panels under `outputs/` (rebuilt, not tracked)

The five-fold runners freeze each run's genotype panels in a `panels.npz` next to its
`cohort.json`, `splits.json` and `selected_features.json`. The Arabidopsis ones
(`outputs/arapheno_study1_ft10/`, `outputs/arapheno_study43/`, the four
`outputs/history/study27/study27_kan_comparison*/` runs and
`outputs/history/transformer/study27_training_selected/`) are subsets of 1001 Genomes
genotypes and are not redistributed. The corresponding runner recreates a panel from the
downloaded inputs and the frozen cohort, split and feature files when it is pointed at that
output directory; the yeast `panels.npz` files (Bloom data) remain tracked.

## 1. 1001 Genomes imputed SNP matrix and kinship (*Arabidopsis thaliana*)

- **Files:** `1001genomes/genotypes/1001_SNP_MATRIX/kinship_ibs_mac5.hdf5` (9.8 MB, IBS kinship, MAC >= 5) and `imputed_snps_binary.hdf5` (919 MB, binary imputed genotypes for 1,135 accessions). Both come from one tarball, `1001_SNP_MATRIX.tar.gz` (332 MB).
- **Source:** <https://1001genomes.org/data/GMI-MPI/releases/v3.1/SNP_matrix_imputed_hdf5/1001_SNP_MATRIX.tar.gz>. The release ships `md5sum.txt` (kinship `d15c69f64c29be8a5caecbce19107eae`, SNP matrix `6857bf13f35d3e36d555958ef33d7b77`).
- **Citation:** 1001 Genomes Consortium. 1,135 Genomes Reveal the Global Pattern of Polymorphism in *Arabidopsis thaliana*. *Cell* 166, 481-491 (2016). doi:10.1016/j.cell.2016.05.063.
- **Terms:** The project's [Data Usage Policy](https://1001genomes.org/policy/) allows free download and use, asks users to acknowledge that the sequence data were produced by the Weigel laboratory at the Max Planck Institute and to cite the primary paper, and states that during the pre-publication waiting period the data "should not be redistributed or repackaged without permission". The genome-wide analysis has since been published, but the files are still not copied into this archive; fetch them from the source.
- **Used by:** rebuilding the Study 27 fixed panel and fresh genome-wide reruns of the Study 1 (FT10), Study 27 and Study 43 analyses need both files. `--kinship-only` skips extracting the 919 MB matrix.

## 2. AraPheno phenotypes (Studies 1, 27 and 43)

- **Files:** `arapheno/bulk/database.zip` (the public per-study export, also linked at `arapheno/database.zip`), the extracted `arapheno/phenotypes/study_{1,43}_{values,phenotypes}.csv`, and the catalog lists `arapheno/metadata/{phenotype_list,study_list}.csv`. Study 27 values are read directly from the export.
- **Source:** <https://arapheno.1001genomes.org/static/database.zip>; <https://arapheno.1001genomes.org/rest/phenotype/list.csv>; <https://arapheno.1001genomes.org/rest/study/list.csv>. The export is regenerated by AraPheno, so its own checksum changes with the export date; the member CSVs are verified instead.
- **Citation (database):** Seren U. et al. AraPheno: a public database for *Arabidopsis thaliana* phenotypes. *Nucleic Acids Res.* 45, D1054-D1059 (2017). doi:10.1093/nar/gkw986. Togninalli M. et al. AraPheno and the AraGWAS Catalog 2020. *Nucleic Acids Res.* 48, D1063-D1068 (2020). doi:10.1093/nar/gkz925.
- **Citation (studies):**
  - Study 1, flowering time at 10 C (FT10, phenotype DOI 10.21958/phenotype:31): Atwell S. et al. Genome-wide association study of 107 phenotypes in *Arabidopsis thaliana* inbred lines. *Nature* 465, 627-631 (2010). doi:10.1038/nature08800.
  - Study 27, mean total root length under control treatment (phenotype DOI 10.21958/phenotype:359): Ristova D. et al. Natural genetic variation shapes root system responses to phytohormones in *Arabidopsis*. *Plant J.* (2018). doi:10.1111/tpj.14034.
  - Study 43, carbon isotope ratio, stomata density and stomata size (e.g. phenotype DOI 10.21958/phenotype:750): Dittberner H. et al. Natural variation in stomata size contributes to the local adaptation of water-use efficiency in *Arabidopsis thaliana*. *Mol. Ecol.* 27, 4052-4065 (2018). doi:10.1111/mec.14838.
- **Terms:** AraPheno publishes no license text. Its [citation page](https://arapheno.1001genomes.org/faq/cite/) asks that each study's original authors be credited through the per-study and per-phenotype DOIs. The whole-database export is therefore downloaded rather than mirrored here.

## 3. Araport11 genome annotation (TAIR)

- **File:** `reference/Araport11_GFF3_genes_transposons.Mar92021.gff.gz` (17.9 MB), used to assign SNPs to genes in the FT10 analyses.
- **Source:** TAIR, Download > Genes > Araport11_genome_release. TAIR serves downloads only to logged-in users (free account), so the script cannot fetch this file; place it at the path above and rerun the script to verify its checksum. A later annotation release (Araport11 20241001) is available under CC BY 4.0 at <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17371665>, but it is not the version used for the archived results.
- **Citation:** Cheng C.-Y. et al. Araport11: a complete reannotation of the *Arabidopsis thaliana* reference genome. *Plant J.* 89, 789-804 (2017). doi:10.1111/tpj.13415. Berardini T. Z. et al. The Arabidopsis Information Resource: making and mining the "gold standard" annotated reference plant genome. *Genesis* 53, 474-485 (2015). doi:10.1002/dvg.22877.
- **Terms:** [TAIR Terms of Use](https://phoenixbioinformatics.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/COM/pages/42216430/TAIR+Terms+of+Use) require attribution by citing a TAIR publication, permit works based on the data to be redistributed, and prohibit redistributing substantial subsets of the data without permission. The annotation file is therefore not mirrored here.

## 4. Bloom lab BYxRM yeast cross

- **Files:** `yeast-byxrm/raw/BYxRM_GenoData.txt` (23.7 MB) and `BYxRM_PhenoData.txt` (0.8 MB): genotypes and averaged phenotypes for 1,008 segregants (Bloom et al. 2013). `yeast-byxrm/raw/Bloom2015_SuppData4_QTLxQTL_interactions.xls` and its CSV conversion `yeast-byxrm/processed/Bloom2015_SuppData4_QTLxQTL_interactions.csv`: the published two-locus interaction list (Bloom et al. 2015, Supplementary Data 4). The CSV is produced by LibreOffice (`soffice --headless --convert-to csv`) and matches the archived file byte for byte.
- **Source:** <http://genomics-pubs.princeton.edu/YeastCross_BYxRM/data.shtml> and the Nature Communications supplementary file <https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fncomms9712/MediaObjects/41467_2015_BFncomms9712_MOESM728_ESM.xls>. Retrieval records with sizes, ETags and checksums are in `yeast-byxrm/MANIFEST.tsv`.
- **Citation:** Bloom J. S. et al. Finding the sources of missing heritability in a yeast cross. *Nature* 494, 234-237 (2013). doi:10.1038/nature11867. Bloom J. S. et al. Genetic interactions contribute less than additive effects to quantitative trait variation in yeast. *Nat. Commun.* 6, 8712 (2015). doi:10.1038/ncomms9712.
- **Terms:** The Princeton data page states no license. The 2015 article and its supplementary files are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.

## Not needed

An accession table (`1001genomes/metadata/accessions.csv`, 1,134 rows) was used during
early data exploration in the development repository. No script in this archive reads
it, so it is neither tracked nor downloaded. The 1001 Genomes and AraPheno accession
lists are available from their web sites.

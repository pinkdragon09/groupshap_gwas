# AraPheno Study 27 matched PC-only RF control

**Note date:** 2026-10-10 16:00:00

The comparison kept a full-phenotype-selected fixed SNP panel and tested RF with
SNPs plus kinship PCs against an otherwise matched PC-only RF. It used all
81 accessions, 200 SNPs, three full-cohort PCs, five shuffled folds with seed 42,
500 trees, `max_features="sqrt"`, `min_samples_leaf=2` and fit seeds 43–47.

`scripts/run_study27_fixed_pc_baseline.py` produced metrics, predictions, paired
comparisons, feature metadata, splits and provenance. Current retained comparison
outputs are under `outputs/history/study27/study27_fixed_pc_baseline/`.

Recorded mean fold R² was 0.5596977102 for SNPs+PCs and -0.0821655032 for PCs only;
their fold standard deviations were 0.1839583710 and 0.5709017640. The mean paired
difference was 0.6418632133. The full model matched a saved RF
reproduction table within 1e-10. It did not exactly reproduce an earlier RF mean
of 0.5587049551: the maximum per-fold discrepancy was 0.0070325753, and its cause
had not been established. The matched PC comparison used the current rerun.

The actual feature count was 200 SNPs even though an earlier loader requested
up to 500. The current archive freezes 81 rows by 203 features in
`data/processed/arapheno_study27/fixed_panel.npz`. Prediction is conditional on
this panel; cross-validation of model fitting does not independently validate
selection that used the full phenotype.

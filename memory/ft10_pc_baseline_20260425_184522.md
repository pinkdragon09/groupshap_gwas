# FT10 and FT16 PC-only RF baseline

**Note date:** 2026-04-25 18:45:22

`scripts/history/ft10_kinship_only_baseline.py` fitted an RF to three kinship
principal components, without SNP predictors, for the FT10 or FT16 phenotype
in AraPheno Study 1.

It reused phenotype loading, kinship-PC construction and cross-validation
constants from `scripts/history/run_ft10_nonlinear.py`. The purpose was to keep
the fitting protocol matched while changing the feature matrix to PCs only.

The phenotype is selected with `--phenotype FT10` or `--phenotype FT16`.
This record describes the baseline implementation, without relying on a separate
task specification or asserting the outcome of the predictive comparison.

# AraPheno Study 1 FT10 five-fold workflow

**Note date:** 2026-09-13 21:39:17

The FT10 workflow used 135 accessions, 200 SNPs selected within training folds,
three PCs fitted on training data and projected to held-out data, five outer folds,
and seeds 42, 43 and 44. It evaluated RF, Transformer and KAN models; RF used
200 trees. These Transformer experiments remain historical records.

The runner is `scripts/run_ft10_fivefold.py`; gene explanations are implemented
in `scripts/ft10_gene_explanations.py`. Numerical results are retained under
`outputs/arapheno_study1_ft10/`. Fold-specific preprocessing and model settings
are saved with the run, rather than inferred from this note.

Gene GroupSHAP explained all 27 held-out accessions in each fold using 16 training
background rows and 128 permutation paths arranged in eight balanced-donor blocks.
It saved signed values, sampling orders, block estimates, Monte Carlo standard
errors and reduced-budget/background diagnostics. Chromosome-level grouped
Shapley values and interactions were enumerated exactly. The workflow also
computed gene/SNP permutation importance, binary ALE and feature-selection frequency.

A PC-only RF control was added during execution. It used the full RF's folds,
preprocessing, seeds and hyperparameters while omitting SNP predictors. It was
not part of the initial fixed main protocol and did not change main-model settings.

All 45 main-model checkpoints and all 15 PC-only RF checkpoints reproduced saved
predictions. Checks covered annotation, sample/background identities, estimator
diagnostics, efficiency and sample gene-permutation calculations. Three grouping
and estimator tests passed. Resume verification successfully skipped the completed
first fold before continuing.

Recorded mean fold R² was 0.3957 for full RF, 0.1000 for Transformer,
0.0030 for KAN, 0.4374 for PC-only RF, 0.2219 for PC-only ridge and -0.1182
for the training-mean predictor. Full RF minus matched PC-only RF was -0.0417.
Performance relative to a linear ridge control does not isolate the SNP contribution.

The union of selected SNPs contained 817 loci. Gene/intergenic player counts,
excluding PCs, were 92, 88, 86, 84 and 17 across folds. A locus or gene absent
from a fold's panel is not evaluated in that fold; absence must not be read as
a biological negative. Sampling diagnostics do not by themselves establish full
rank convergence. Checkpoint verification after cloning requires restored model
files or retraining.

Selection frequencies were 710 SNPs in one fold, 54 in two, 31 in three,
21 in four and one in all five. FLC had physical 25 kb window coverage only
in fold 4 but was never assigned a nearest-gene player. FRI, FT and VIN3 were
absent. DOG1 had physical coverage in folds 4 and 5 and a gene-player assignment
only in fold 4. These are panel-coverage observations.
